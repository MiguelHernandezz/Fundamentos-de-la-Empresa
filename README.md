## 📘 What the subject covers

> **Fundamentos de la Empresa (781001)** · 6 ECTS · 1st year · 2nd semester · contents from the 2024-25 teaching guide · [official subject page](https://www.uah.es/es/estudios/estudios-oficiales/grados/asignatura/Fundamentos-de-la-Empresa-781001/)

| Unit | Hours | Topics |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction to business economics | 6 h | The company as an organisation and a system · Types of companies, the entrepreneur · Writing a business plan: legal form, size, location, tax, financial plan |
| The company as a socio-economic institution | 6 h | Management functions and strategy · Objectives, competitiveness and globalisation · Value creation and productivity |
| Business decisions | 9 h | Production factors and production costs · Break-even point · Investment appraisal: NPV, IRR, payback · Financing sources and working capital |
| Business information systems: conceptual foundations | 9 h | – |
| The accounting cycle | 6 h | – |
| Commercial operations, their recording and the financial statements | 9 h | – |
